INSIDE THE SYSTEM

Intelligent matching.
Coordinated execution.

Flex connects what travellers need with what others can change. AI interprets their conditions, an allocation engine finds compatible arrangements, and a transaction system carries them through.

Structured intent and conditional rules+

DeepSeek and Gemini adapters interpret language into typed rules linked to booking records. Schema validation checks the fields, account references and source phrases before the traveller reviews the result.

“When I fly alone for up to six hours, any seat is fine.”

Solo journeyANDFlight ≤ 6 hours→Any seat acceptable

A compositional rule model combines conditions, alternatives and exceptions with AND, OR and NOT. A three-valued evaluator distinguishes satisfied, unsatisfied and unknown conditions. Required outcomes remain binding, while softer preferences contribute to allocation quality.

Conditional order book and allocation method+

The conditional order book holds published requests, saved flexibility and their current status. Candidate generation searches exchange cycles, chains and service bundles, checking the complete outcome against each traveller’s conditions.

Branch-and-bound prunes combinations that cannot improve the best result. Selection first maximises newly completed requests, then the net gain in satisfied standing preferences. Further ties favour higher stress-adjusted operational value, lower new credit issuance and fewer booking changes, in that order. Capacity, reserved funds and booking versions constrain selection. Accepted passenger prices stay fixed.

Candidate generation searches seat cycles and chains of up to four travellers and combines up to three compatible parts. Each result records its candidate coverage, search bound and completion status.

Idempotent execution and recovery+

Versioned reservations keep the plan tied to the current booking. A persisted execution record tracks each service update and receipt. Stable command identities make retries idempotent, and verified fulfilment creates one final ledger entry.

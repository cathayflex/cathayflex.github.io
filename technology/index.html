01 · UNDERSTAND

Turn travel needs
into clear conditions.

AI drafts the traveller’s requirements and connects them to the right booking. The traveller reviews the result before it can guide a change.

A TRAVEL REQUESTPrepared interpretation
“For Tokyo, can the three of us sit together? We also need one extra checked bag.”
Which journey?CX 520 · Hong Kong to TokyoResolved from the account’s booking
Who sits together?Lin, Mia and JamieThree consecutive seats in one row and one seat block
Which baggage product?One extra checked bag · up to 23 kgAirline inventory, assigned to the travelling passenger

An interpretation is a draft. It cannot spend credits or change a booking.

Inspect the interpretation contract+

“Only if”, “either” and “except” become explicit conditions. Missing information stays unknown. A hard requirement continues to apply when a softer preference is added.

Each rule stores its source excerpt, scope, condition, effect and strength. Conditions support bounded AND, OR and NOT trees. A separate outcome condition can make a voluntary concession depend on the complete arrangement.

Source evidenceA verbatim excerpt anchors every rule. Structural validation catches missing or fabricated excerpts. Semantic accuracy still needs user review.
Account contextJourney and party identifiers must exist in the permitted booking context. A shared booking does not establish a family relationship.
UncertaintyUnknown facts do not satisfy permission conditions. Ambiguity produces a question. Unsupported outcomes remain unresolved.
Bounded complexityUp to 8 levels and 64 nodes per condition tree, with up to 16 branches per group. Oversized input is rejected before recursive evaluation.
Model boundaryDeepSeek and Gemini adapters produce structured drafts. The model receives no authority to publish, reserve inventory or settle a transaction.

02 · MODEL

Bring requests and
resources together.

Flex keeps bookings, saved flexibility, published requests and reservations in one model. Each published request has an agreed outcome, a fixed price and an expiry.

THE SHARED STATEOne consistent snapshot for matching
Bookings
Existing seats, travelling companions and available inventory.
Flexibility
Conditions under which a traveller is open to an offer.
Published requests
Required outcomes with an accepted quote and deadline.
Reservations
Resources and credits already committed to pending arrangements.

New requests, withdrawals, expiry and inventory changes update this model.

A conditional request book. Saved flexibility guides proposals. A published request authorizes its exact terms. Keeping them distinct prevents a preference from becoming an accidental purchase.

Inspect the formal model and its boundaries+

The book is a deterministic projection of the current platform state. It separates indicative permissions, authorized requests and contracts in progress. It assigns no trading priority of its own.

Choose complete arrangementsmaximize the declared policy objectivesubject to permissions, capacity, funding and consent
Decision variablesOne binary variable selects each complete candidate contract. Compatible selected contracts must remain independently reservable.
Hard conditionsEvery required predicate must hold in the projected outcome. Unknown facts cannot supply permission. An incomplete service bundle cannot satisfy an all-or-nothing request.
Shared constraintsSelected debits fit each wallet and exact request earmark. Resource demand fits capacity after current allocations and holds. Conflicting candidates cannot both be selected.
Time and changeA new request, cancellation, expiry or inventory update changes the snapshot. Candidates and dependencies are revalidated before reservation and execution.
Market designThe current product uses fixed platform quotes and constrained combination selection. It has no passenger bidding, continuous price discovery or price-time matching priority.
Value measurementThe current objective uses declared model scores and synthetic operating values. Real incremental value must be measured against a comparable baseline using fulfilment, waiting, disruption, cost and credit liability.

Inspect the optimization model+

A family arrangement is checked after every proposed seat move, including companions who stay in place. Guardian adjacency, eligibility and conditional permissions must all hold. Separate offers can still compete for the same seat or credits.

A candidate is a complete proposed contract. A binary decision selects or excludes each candidate. Bounded branch-and-bound compares compatible subsets in a fixed priority order.

  1. Model benefit scoreSum of the candidate gain values under the current policy.
  2. Stress-adjusted operational valueModeled value less service cost, risk and stressed credit cost, represented in integer minor units.
  3. Lower credit issuancePrefer a smaller new credit liability when the first two totals tie.

These are explicit policy coefficients. They are not measured passenger utility. Fixed passenger quotes do not change when the optimization score changes.

FeasibilityAllocation versions, read/write conflicts, physical seats, shared wallet debits, protected capacity and issuance budgets constrain selection. Releases in a different uncommitted contract cannot fund capacity.
Reserved creditsGeneral spending uses the available wallet balance. A published request has a separate funding allocation, usable only by a candidate covered by that exact authorization. Multiple plans cannot spend the same reserved amount.
Generated domainThe current generator explores seat cycles and empty-inventory chains up to four participants, joint party moves and bundles of up to three parts. Default generation budgets are 20,000 work visits and 192 candidates.
Search boundsA feasible incumbent is retained when the node budget runs out. The result reports an upper bound and the first unresolved objective tier.
Scope of optimalityA completed selection search proves the best subset of the generated candidates under this policy. Candidate generation is bounded. This does not establish a globally optimal airline network allocation.

04 · CONFIRM

Confirm the agreement.
Verify every change.

A published request already authorizes its exact terms. Other affected travellers choose whether to participate. Credits settle after every required change is confirmed.

01

Check current terms

Verify the accepted quote, deadline and booking versions. Reserve the complete arrangement before asking for the remaining consent.

02

Collect remaining consent

Each affected traveller reviews their proposed change. The requester’s existing authorization covers the outcome they already agreed to.

03

Verify and settle

The platform confirms every resource change before posting the credit ledger. Repeating a command cannot issue the reward twice.

What if a booking system does not respond?

A timeout can mean that a change happened but its acknowledgement was lost. Flex keeps the reservation, checks authoritative booking state and resumes reconciliation. Credits settle only after every target is verified.

The implemented adapter simulates these failures. Real airline execution requires provider-specific reservation, reconciliation and operational escalation.

Inspect consent, expiry and recovery+
Before publicationThe server recomputes the fixed quote against the reviewed draft. A changed price, product, booking or request revision invalidates the quote.
While matchingThe requester has already agreed within the published terms. Matching does not ask them to approve the same outcome again. Other participants may still need to accept.
High-impact changesA flight-time offer becomes actionable after the other required confirmations. A tentative arrangement never instructs a passenger to change their plans.
Before commitmentExpiry closes the request. Allocation versions and dependencies are checked again to reject stale offers. Withdrawals cannot silently undo a reserved arrangement.
After commitmentAn expiry cannot interrupt an in-progress booking write. Funds and resources remain isolated through reconciliation.
SettlementConfirmed allocation targets and required delivery evidence precede settlement. Each contract has at most one ledger entry. Issuance and redemption reconcile with wallet balances.

Inspect measured search results+

Reproducible synthetic selection tests compare complete searches with an independent exhaustive subset oracle on small markets. Separate tests check identity renaming, storage order, joint family moves and complete four-traveller seat assignments.

Loading the measured results…

A separate state-machine test runs 48 fixed seeds with 440 scheduled commands, then replays every command. It compares intermediate states and checks wallet changes against newly settled ledger entries. The sequences cover races, expiry, repeated commands and simulated write failures.

These are individual local measurements, including initialization and runtime effects. They establish the recorded run’s behavior, not production latency or a global airline optimum.

What runs here Matching, validation and the credit ledger are computed from platform code. Language interpretations, bookings and airline execution are prepared or simulated.

Engineering references and reproducibility+

The architecture combines language interpretation with deterministic validation, constrained selection and verified execution. These references inform the design. They do not establish validation of this implementation.

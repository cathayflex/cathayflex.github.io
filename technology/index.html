How language becomes rules+

Each rule preserves the original phrase, when it applies, what it changes and whether it is a requirement or a preference. AI proposes this structure. Account records resolve the flight, travelling party and available products.

A CONDITIONAL PERMISSION

“When I fly alone for up to six hours, any seat is fine.”

Travelling aloneANDFlight ≤ 6 hours→Any seat acceptable
Conditions
AND combines requirements. OR admits alternatives. NOT expresses an exception. Each check evaluates to true, false or unknown. Missing booking facts produce a clarification.
Scope and strength
Rules can apply across journeys or to a specific booking. Hard requirements remain binding when softer preferences are added. A condition can depend on the complete proposed outcome.
Source and context
Each rule carries its source excerpt. Journey and party identifiers are checked against the account. A shared booking supplies companion context, with relationship details resolved separately.
Execution format
DeepSeek and Gemini adapters return structured drafts. Deterministic validation checks the schema, source evidence and account context. Condition trees allow 8 levels, 64 nodes and 16 branches per group.

The request book
A versioned snapshot brings together bookings, inventory, saved flexibility, active requests and reservations. Flexibility guides proposals. Published requests authorize their exact outcome, fixed quote and expiry.
Complete candidates
Seat cycles and chains connect up to four travellers. Bundles combine up to three parts. Joint party checks evaluate the final seating plan, including companions who keep their seats. Guardian adjacency and seat eligibility are evaluated with that plan.
Selection
Each candidate has a binary selection variable. Bounded branch-and-bound compares compatible subsets. Constraints cover conflicting seat assignments, allocation versions, shared wallet debits, request earmarks, inventory capacity and the airline’s reward budget.
Objective
The selector first maximizes the policy’s model benefit score, then stress-adjusted operational value, then minimizes new credit issuance. Operational values include service cost, risk and stressed credit cost in integer minor units. Passenger quotes remain fixed.
Search result
A completed search establishes the best combination within the supplied candidate set. At a work limit, the engine returns a feasible selection and an upper bound on the objective. Candidate generation and selection have separate completion records.
Changing conditions
Requests, withdrawals, expiry and inventory updates change the snapshot. The engine rechecks the chosen arrangement before reservation and execution. Each request keeps its accepted price throughout its active period.

How agreement and settlement work+
Request authorization
Publishing accepts a specific outcome, line-item quote, booking revision and deadline. A matching arrangement uses this recorded authorization. Other affected travellers consent to their own proposed changes.
Reservation
The request earmarks credits at publication. The selected arrangement reserves its resources before collecting the remaining consent. Expiry releases an unused earmark and closes the request.
Execution and recovery
Seat and service adapters record their progress separately. If an acknowledgement is lost, the arrangement keeps its reservations while the system reads authoritative booking state and completes reconciliation.
Settlement
Every required target is verified before the ledger posts. Stable command identities make retries repeatable. Each contract settles once, then releases its reservations.
Booking write→Acknowledgement lost→Read booking state→Resume and settle

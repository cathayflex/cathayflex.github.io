# Algorithm frontiers for Cathay Flex

Research and code review completed on 4 October 2026.

The next useful algorithm is a bounded assignment model that constructs complete arrangements directly from verified bookings, permissions, party relationships and available inventory. The current implementation already separates candidate generation, complete-outcome validation, fixed quotes and compatible selection. Its main remaining search limitation is the set of arrangements it can generate. Improving selection alone cannot recover an arrangement that never entered that set.

This is a focused literature synthesis and engineering recommendation. It is not a systematic review, a new algorithm claim, or evidence of an airline deployment. Source claims below are limited to the inspected publications and official documentation. Proposed airline applications and evaluation targets are identified as recommendations.

## What the implemented system establishes

The code reviewed here is the current local implementation, including the changes accompanying this note.

| Layer | Implemented behavior | Boundary that matters |
|---|---|---|
| Interpretation | `agent.ts` produces reviewable drafts with source evidence. `predicates.ts` validates nested AND, OR and NOT with at most eight levels, 64 nodes and 16 children per Boolean group. | Syntactic validity and a matching source excerpt cannot establish semantic fidelity to every clause. |
| Conditional meaning | `intent.ts` preserves unknown facts under negation and retains standing hard restrictions. Conditional service permissions can require a complete proposed seat, party, baggage or departure outcome. | `onlyIf` currently applies to flexible permissions. Unsupported dependent hard goals and arrival or delivery guarantees require clarification. |
| Candidate generation | `generic-market.ts` searches seat cycles and chains up to length four, composes some joint party moves, and builds bundles of up to three parts. Defaults bound work at 20,000 visits and output at 192 candidates. | Completing this procedure means completing its declared search domain. It does not enumerate every feasible full allocation. |
| Validation | `engine.ts` checks complete projected allocations, including flight and seat consistency and conditional outcomes. Dependencies include unchanged companions and services used by a promise. | Validation proves the encoded arrangement against a snapshot. External facts and adapter capabilities still need authoritative evidence. |
| Selection | `selection.ts` uses a greedy incumbent and bounded branch-and-bound with conflict-clique upper bounds. It checks shared capacity, wallets, campaign budgets, conflicting writes, shared reads and reward events. | Its optimality and gap refer to supplied candidates and its declared objective. The default `gain` score is a model coefficient, not measured passenger utility or a count of completed requests. |
| Pricing and execution | Fixed service quotes are separate from matching. Exact accepted terms bind spending. Holds and reconciliation protect uncertain execution. | The workspace simulates airline operations. It does not establish access to airline reservation systems or cross-system atomic writes. |

The exhaustive selector oracle checks subsets of supplied synthetic candidates. A separate generator test now enumerates every seat permutation in one four-traveller family market and compares the feasible assignments with generated outcomes. These establish selection correctness on the tested instances and generation recall in that small seat domain. The next experiment extends assignment coverage across market structures, service categories and conditional branches.

## Why several research traditions are needed

### Complete arrangements behave like combinatorial allocation

A family arrangement may consume several seats, a baggage entitlement, a campaign event and a wallet reservation together. Selecting it can exclude several individually attractive alternatives. Sandholm’s winner-determination formulation and search methods describe this allocation structure through mutually compatible packages and admissible search bounds. Flex can use that mathematics while keeping quoted prices fixed and separately defining the selection objective. The source does not imply that passengers must bid. [Sandholm, 2006](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~sandholm/www/windetalgs.pdf). <!--ref:sandholm2006--><!--anchor:section:1%20and%202.2.2-->

The present selector follows this structure. Its next useful improvement depends on profiling. Conflict decomposition, tighter bounds and cached compatibility can reduce selection effort. None of them resolves missing complete outcomes. Moreover, decomposing by flight is unsafe when a shared wallet, party, campaign or cross-flight condition couples those components. Generation can partition locally while a global master still enforces shared quantities.

### Exchange cycles provide structure, while party conditions require more

Dickerson, Manlove, Plaut, Sandholm and Trimble study position-indexed formulations for bounded exchange cycles and chains, with extensions for failure-aware exchange. Their formulation is relevant when an occupied seat moves through several willing travellers or an empty seat terminates a chain. The kidney-exchange problem supplies algorithms and a useful comparison domain. Its medical priorities, donor constraints and failure assumptions do not transfer to passenger travel. [Dickerson et al., 2016](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/119356/). <!--ref:dickerson2016--><!--anchor:section:Abstract-->

For Flex, a cycle edge is only local admissibility. A party requirement may become true after several simultaneous moves even though no isolated move satisfies it. The implemented generator now retains some such fragments until it checks their union. A complete assignment model would represent the entire affected party and every fixed companion from the outset, reducing dependence on hand-selected cycle and bundle templates.

### Boolean implications fit an assignment model

OR-Tools documents channeling through implication and half-reified constraints. That directly supports the meaning of a later flight implying an aisle and a baggage entitlement. The converse requires a separate relation. Its solver documentation also distinguishes a feasible result from proven optimality, proven infeasibility and an unresolved search. Those distinctions should survive any adapter into Flex. [Google channeling documentation](https://developers.google.com/optimization/cp/channeling), [Google CP-SAT documentation](https://developers.google.com/optimization/cp/cp_solver). <!--ref:ortools-channeling--><!--anchor:section:If-Then-Else%20expressions--><!--ref:ortools-status--><!--anchor:section:CP-SAT%20return%20values-->

A proposed model uses passenger-to-seat assignment variables, selected-flight variables, personal entitlement quantities and Boolean variables for complete branches. It includes keeping the existing arrangement, exact capacity, permitted service changes, verified party adjacency and flight-to-seat compatibility. Unknown facts remove unsupported permissions or require clarification before the corresponding hard promise can be offered. A solver must never turn missing booking context into an assumed solo trip.

### Generate useful columns and separate violated constraints

Column generation works with a restricted master and a subproblem that supplies additional useful columns. Lübbecke and Desrosiers explain its decomposition and dual perspective. This is relevant if feasible bundles become too numerous to enumerate. The pricing subproblem here would discover an arrangement that improves the optimization model. Its name does not mean changing a passenger’s fixed commercial quote. [Lübbecke and Desrosiers, 2005](https://or.rwth-aachen.de/files/research/publications/cgsurvey.pdf). <!--ref:lubbecke2005--><!--anchor:section:2-->

Lazy constraints address a different problem. Gurobi documents adding constraints that eliminate invalid candidate solutions during mixed-integer search. This can help when checking every complex relationship upfront is expensive. It requires a sound separation procedure that rejects every violating incumbent relevant to correctness. It does not establish that ungenerated variables are unnecessary. [Gurobi lazy-constraint documentation](https://support.gurobi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013197972-How-do-I-implement-lazy-constraints-in-Gurobi). <!--ref:gurobi-lazy--><!--anchor:paragraph:1-->

Recommendation follows from this distinction. Start with an explicit bounded assignment model and an exhaustive small-instance oracle. Add column generation only after measurements show candidate explosion, and only with a documented pricing oracle. LP column-generation convergence alone does not certify an integer optimum. Do not describe a CP-SAT feasibility callback as a general branch-and-price implementation.

### Time and uncertainty can matter more than a larger search

Akbarpour, Li and Oveis Gharan show that the benefit of waiting depends on what a planner knows about agents’ departure. Their model can strongly favor waiting when critical departures are observable, while greedy matching performs relatively well without that information. Their analysis uses a particular stochastic matching model, including compatibility assumptions that exclude important forms of persistent heterogeneity. It does not prove that delaying an airline offer is beneficial. [Akbarpour et al., 2020](https://web.stanford.edu/~mohamwad/DynamicMatching.pdf). <!--ref:akbarpour2020--><!--anchor:section:IV%20and%20V-->

A Flex experiment should compare immediate matching with short periodic batches and deadline-aware rolling replanning. Freeze accepted or executing arrangements. Evaluate only unsigned opportunities as new inventory and requests arrive. Missed service cutoffs, passenger waiting and offer churn must count as costs. This is a proposed experiment, with no measured benefit yet.

Bertsimas and Sim formalize a tunable tradeoff between nominal objective quality and protection against uncertain data, including discrete optimization. Applying that idea requires a specified uncertainty set and a defensible meaning for protection. Their results cannot turn an unknown passenger acceptance probability into reliable airline evidence. [Bertsimas and Sim, 2004](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.1030.0065). <!--ref:bertsimas2004--><!--anchor:section:Abstract-->

The first useful uncertainty study is scenario replay over documented capacity changes, expiry timing and adapter latency. Compare nominal plans with plans that reserve recovery time or avoid excessive coupling. Report both completed value and forgone feasible value. An arbitrary scalar called risk is not a calibrated robustness model. Hard permissions, accepted quote amounts and funding obligations must stay fixed in every scenario.

### Recovery and language correctness remain independent obligations

Sagas organize long-lived work into subtransactions with compensation for partial execution. Gray and Lamport analyze distributed commit and the relation between two-phase commit and fault-tolerant consensus. These results explain why local bookkeeping and a timeout cannot establish the result of an external booking write. Compensating an airline change may require a funded remedy when the original seat is no longer recoverable. [Garcia-Molina and Salem, 1987](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/research/techreps/598), [Gray and Lamport, 2006](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/consensus-on-transaction-commit/). <!--ref:garciamolina1987--><!--anchor:section:Abstract--><!--ref:gray2006--><!--anchor:section:Publication-->

The simulated engine now revalidates complete outcomes before reconciliation resumes. Its validation view restores the contract's changed allocations to their original rights while retaining current values for unchanged dependencies. A regression moves unchanged companions after a partial seat write and verifies that reconciliation stays blocked, funds remain held and no settlement occurs. This protects the encoded promise without discarding adapter progress.

Extend fault injection across durable reservation, external write, receipt recording and ledger settlement. Assert that an uncertain write remains locked, retries reuse the same operation identity, duplicate receipts cannot settle twice and changed terms require renewed consent. These are repository validation goals, with external provider guarantees still requiring separate evidence.

LINC assigns language interpretation to a model and formal inference to a symbolic prover. Its reported results and complementary failure modes support testing those components separately. The paper evaluates logical reasoning benchmarks, not airline consent or payment authorization. A correct solver can still enforce an incorrectly translated passenger statement. [Olausson et al., 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.313/). <!--ref:olausson2023--><!--anchor:section:Abstract-->

The proposed semantic benchmark should therefore score clause omission, reversed implication, hard-to-soft weakening, entity binding, unsupported guarantees and unknown-under-negation separately from JSON validity. Human-confirmed statements remain the authority for intended meaning. Models can draft interpretations, explain verified results and propose clarification. They do not acquire execution authority by generating a well-formed formula.

## A live request book connects changing needs to execution

A request book makes the current market inspectable. Its useful abstraction is a person, journey, resource rights, requested outcome, permission conditions, accepted quote, validity period and revision. Seats, personal baggage and departure options can share that structure while retaining their own feasibility rules. Changing a need changes the recorded terms and the arrangements those terms support. Language interpretation supplies a reviewed draft of this model. It cannot create inventory, consent or funding.

The `projectOrderBook(state)` design derives requests, permissions, contracts and wallet reservations from authoritative state at a recorded version and simulation hour. Reading the book cannot place an order or run an exchange. Its categories carry different obligations.

| Entry | Meaning for matching and execution |
|---|---|
| Indicative flexibility | A traveller describes acceptable changes. A supplier still needs to accept a concrete arrangement unless separate request authority covers it. |
| Draft or invalidated request | Terms are unresolved, unconfirmed or no longer consistent with the accepted quote. They cannot authorize a new arrangement. |
| Open, firm request | A current fixed quote and available reserved funding can authorize a new complete arrangement within its terms. This says nothing about whether the required supply or other consent is available. |
| Reserved or executing request | Real contract identifiers carry the pending obligation. It is unavailable for another new commitment. An uncertain write retains its reservations. |
| Settled, fulfilled, expired or withdrawn request | The book reports the actual reason it is closed. Fulfilment observed outside a recorded contract must remain distinguishable from verified settlement. |

Financial order books supply useful lifecycle discipline, while their allocation policies need separate justification. Coinbase describes a continuous exchange with price-time execution priority. Budish, Cramton and Shim analyze a financial-market alternative using discrete batch auctions. Flex instead selects compatible, indivisible arrangements at already accepted prices. One family request can require several people, seats and a bag together. Sorting its display by time would establish neither FIFO execution nor fairness. Its search certificate covers the supplied candidate set. [Coinbase matching-engine documentation](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/exchange/concepts/matching-engine), [Budish et al., 2015](https://www.econ.umd.edu/publication/high-frequency-trading-arms-race-frequent-batch-auctions-market-design-response-0). <!--ref:coinbase-matching--><!--anchor:section:Exchange%20Matching%20Engine--><!--ref:budish2015--><!--anchor:section:Abstract-->

### What transfers from Jane Street's public engineering account

Brian Nigito's exchange talk describes explicit order and cancel messages, a clean deterministic matching state machine, ordered output, replay and separate reporting applications. It also identifies shared constraints as a reason to be careful when partitioning by instrument. The useful transfer to Flex is a small, typed transition boundary with derived views outside it. Shared wallets, parties and cross-resource promises must remain checked together. This is an architectural comparison to a public talk. It establishes no claim about implementing Jane Street's proprietary system, performance or reliability. [Jane Street, How to Build an Exchange](https://www.janestreet.com/tech-talks/building-an-exchange/). <!--ref:janestreet-exchange--><!--anchor:section:Transcript-->

Jane Street's testing account combines simulated network and time, generated event sequences, version-skew tests, fuzzing and more realistic fault environments. Its education account connects property tests, controllable time and safer typed APIs. These suggest a concrete Flex experiment described below. The testing article also discloses Jane Street's investment in Antithesis. It is engineering experience with a disclosed commercial interest, with no comparative evidence that purchasing that service is necessary for Flex. [Patti, 2025](https://blog.janestreet.com/getting-from-tested-to-battle-tested/), [Bauer, 2024](https://blog.janestreet.com/developer-education-at-jane-street-index/). <!--ref:janestreet-testing--><!--anchor:section:Testing%20everything%20you%20can%20think%20of--><!--ref:janestreet-education--><!--anchor:section:The%20Dev%20Teach-in%20curriculum-->

### Lifecycle messages must describe completed state changes

FIX distinguishes a received cancellation request from a completed cancellation, including cases where execution wins the race. This is relevant to Flex's withdrawal-versus-reservation race even though Flex implements its own protocol. AWS's idempotent API account likewise distinguishes a retry with the same identity from a changed request reusing that identity. [FIX Order State Changes, 2020](https://www.fixtrading.org/wp-content/uploads/download-manager-files/Order-State-Changes.pdf), [AWS Builders' Library](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/making-retries-safe-with-idempotent-APIs/). <!--ref:fix-order-states--><!--anchor:section:Order%20State%20Changes--><!--ref:aws-idempotence--><!--anchor:section:Same%20client%20request%20ID%2C%20different%20intent-->

Flex currently checks command identity and expected state version, conditions the database update on its previous version, and verifies allocation dependencies before commitment and recovery. An unsuccessful cancellation must leave the reservation visible. A timeout cannot release an uncertain booking change. These safeguards and passing race tests are narrower than a proof of linearizability, which requires each completed operation to fit a legal sequential history consistent with real-time order. A derived book adds no such proof or distributed commit guarantee. [Herlihy and Wing, 1990](https://cs.brown.edu/~mph/HerlihyW90/p463-herlihy.pdf). <!--ref:herlihy1990--><!--anchor:section:1.1%20Overview-->

### Measure additional complete outcomes and their costs

The value hypothesis is that structured needs expose compatible choices that isolated booking changes miss. Lin's request becomes achievable when Daniel's acceptable seat move and airline baggage inventory form one executable arrangement. That synthetic example illustrates the mechanism. It does not estimate airline demand, passenger welfare or realized savings. Transferring 160 credits from one wallet to another creates no aggregate credit balance, and issuing credits creates a future obligation.

Evaluate this hypothesis on identical arrival, inventory, expiry and consent traces. Compare unchanged bookings, individual inventory redemption, pairwise exchanges, the current complete-bundle matcher and a tiny exhaustive assignment oracle. Keep accepted prices, hard restrictions, funding and service deadlines constant. Count each fully delivered request once. Report completion before cutoff, waiting, number of booking changes, unresolved recovery time, service cost, avoided operational cost and outstanding credit liability separately. If operational net value is estimated, publish the cost model and subtract execution costs and incremental liability once. Passenger utility needs independent evidence and cannot be inferred from `gain` or the number of credits transferred.

The implemented seeded history runner now exercises 48 seeds and 440 scheduled commands, then replays all 440 commands and compares every intermediate state hash. Sixteen coverage categories include cancellation and acceptance races, expiry, stale versions, duplicate and reused command identities, simulated write failures and recovery. Independent assertions reconcile each wallet delta with new ledger entries, protect historical ledger entries and enforce one settlement per contract. This establishes the tested local transition histories.

The next extension should vary publication, revision and inventory events more broadly, compare the request-book projection during replay and shrink failing sequences to minimal reproductions. It should also exercise simultaneous identical HTTP submissions with a response lost after the database commit. Use controlled arrival traces for the allocation baselines above, reporting completed outcomes and failure counts before claiming a benefit. These experiments extend the pure transitions and focused regression tests without requiring a new exchange service or an unverified generalization of the matching domain.

## The next implementation and its acceptance test

Implement an offline assignment oracle for one verified market component before adding another production service. Feed it the same typed state, fixed quote and rules as the current engine. Return complete `Candidate` objects that still pass the existing validator. Start with seats, party adjacency and personal baggage. Add operating-flight changes only when the state represents the required inventory association and service compatibility explicitly.

1. Define a versioned supported domain with a bound on travellers, seats, party size, service products and Boolean nodes. Reject unsupported predicates visibly.
2. Enumerate all complete allocations for tiny instances through an independent reference routine. Use this to measure candidate recall and catch invalid pruning.
3. Build a compact CP-SAT or equivalent integer assignment prototype. Keep current arrangements as explicit choices and impose conditional branches before optimizing.
4. Compare its complete outcomes with the current cycle, chain and bundle generator under equal compute budgets. Feed both outputs to the same fixed-quote validator and selector.
5. Retain a feasible incumbent at the deadline and report the modeled domain, unresolved search and bound. A certificate must state whether it covers the assignment model or only a supplied candidate set.

The acceptance criterion is increased recall of valid complete outcomes on unseen synthetic structures while preserving zero hard-condition, capacity, funding and consent violations. A small latency increase may be justified if it recovers materially more complete requests. That tradeoff needs measured results and a product latency budget.

| Experiment | Baselines and ablations | Measurements |
|---|---|---|
| Generation correctness | Exhaustive tiny allocations, current bounded generator, assignment prototype. Remove joint outcome checks only in an intentionally invalid ablation. | Valid candidate recall, false feasible outcomes, completed requests, unsupported-domain count. |
| Selection quality | Greedy only, current branch-and-bound, exhaustive subset oracle, established solver on identical columns. | Lexicographic objective, first unresolved tier, certified gap, explored nodes, deterministic replay. |
| Scalability | Vary travellers, seats, graph density, party size, Boolean depth, service categories and budget coupling independently. | Generation and selection p50/p95/p99 latency, peak memory, cutoff rate and quality at cutoff. |
| Online policy | Immediate matching, fixed batching, deadline-aware replanning. Repeat with reliable and unreliable departure information. | Completion before cutoff, waiting, offer churn, expired holds, regret against a hindsight benchmark. |
| Robustness and recovery | Nominal replay, protected recovery margins, correlated inventory loss and delayed or duplicate receipts. | Verified completion, unresolved duration, compensation cost, double-settlement count, resources released while uncertain. |
| AI contribution | Structured forms, model interpretation plus deterministic matching, optional model candidate suggestions. | Clause fidelity, clarification burden, valid outcome recall, invalid suggestion rate and added latency. |

Use seeded generators with held-out seeds and adversarial examples. Include a five-person cycle outside the current length limit, a party requiring two jointly feasible moves, two offers competing for one empty seat, shared wallets across journeys, read dependencies changed by another contract, and a conditional seat move whose baggage is only provided by the complete bundle. Publish the corpus construction and instance dimensions. A visually successful demonstration is not a performance evaluation.

## Verification scope and remaining gaps

Sources were discovered by targeted searches and inspected on author or institutional repositories, publisher pages and official solver documentation. The included records cover foundational allocation and systems results, established optimization methods and a 2023 language-to-logic study. Publication metadata was cross-checked where the author copy and publisher or institutional record were available. Web crawl dates were not treated as publication years.

The evidence types are theoretical results, algorithmic evaluations, systems designs and implementation documentation. A clinical evidence hierarchy would misclassify these sources. No systematic retraction search, proprietary benchmark access or comprehensive conflict-of-interest audit was performed. Solver documentation describes vendor-supported capabilities and provides no independent evidence that a particular solver will outperform this implementation. The reviewed papers’ results remain bounded by their own models and experiments.

Two central gaps remain empirical. We do not yet know whether complete assignment search materially improves fulfilment under realistic, consent-constrained airline inventory. We also lack measured adapter failure and recovery distributions. The proposed tests address the first gap with independent synthetic oracles and clearly bounded claims. Authoritative airline integration and controlled operational evidence are needed for the second.

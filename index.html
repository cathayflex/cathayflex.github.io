01 What is Flex

Different travellers.
Different priorities.

Exchange what you can change for what matters more to you, on this journey or the next.

One traveller needs an earlier flight. Another can leave later, but would value extra baggage on a future trip. Their priorities differ, and both can benefit from a change.

Flex finds these opportunities across travellers and airline services. It brings the changes into one offer that everyone involved can review and accept.

SEAT FOR SEAT

Let Flex find a seat
exchange that suits you.

You have a window seat and prefer the aisle. Another traveller wants the reverse. Flex offers a coordinated seat change that gives both of you a better fit. The platform handles the offer, consent and booking updates, including exchanges across several travellers.

What each person getsA preferred seat. Any credit adjustment is shown in the platform’s offer and settled by Flex.

A MEAL CHOICE FOR FUTURE VALUE

Make an early choice.
Use the reward later.

Already planning to eat before you fly? If Cathay offers a reward for skipping an onboard meal, you can accept before the catering deadline. The confirmed change helps Cathay plan what to prepare.

What you receiveFlex credits you can use towards an available seat or service, including on a future trip.

03 · FLEX CREDITS

Give on one trip.
Receive on another.

Flex can coordinate an exchange within one journey. Credits let you accept a change now and choose a different benefit later, through the same platform.

Cathay funds rewards for changes that have value to the airline. Flex manages each offer and settles the agreed credits through the platform. You use your balance to book available seats and services through Flex.

  1. EarnComplete an agreed exchange

    The offer states your reward. Credits become available once the full exchange is completed.

  2. KeepCarry value in your Flex balance

    You can give up one service now and use the reward for a different service later.

  3. UseChoose something you need

    See the seats and services available for your trip, check their credit prices and choose what to book.

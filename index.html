FLEX CREDITS

Credits make more
exchanges possible.

The traveller you help may want something different from you. Credits let you contribute through one resource and benefit through another, across services and journeys.

For Cathay, passenger flexibility opens more ways to fulfil demand within existing capacity.

Seat and flight changes

Earn for accepting a change. Spend to request one.

Checked baggage and meals

Earn by releasing unused allowance or declining a meal early. Use credits for extra baggage.

Upgrades and neighbour-free seats

Reserve with credits. Pay when confirmed.

Lounge access and Wi-Fi

Redeem access at a fixed credit price.

YOUR CHOICES

Clear terms.
You stay in control.

Every offer shows the exact change and reward. Every request has an agreed price and deadline.

You choose the offers you accept and the terms of your requests. Track each arrangement and see when your booking and balance are updated.

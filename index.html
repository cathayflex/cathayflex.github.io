THE IDEA

Turn your flexibility into
the changes you need.

Flex connects travellers who need a change with people willing to make one. Agree to a suitable change, earn credits, then use them to request a flight time, seat or service that matters to you.

FLEX CREDITS

Credits make more
exchanges possible.

The traveller you help doesn’t need to offer what you need in return. Flex credits connect separate exchanges through one balance.

One shared unit
Bring different eligible changes together through one credit balance.
Value carried forward
Earning and using credits can happen at different times, as your travel needs change.

For Cathay, this creates more ways to meet passenger needs using the capacity already available.

YOUR CHOICES

Clear terms.
You stay in control.

See the proposed booking changes, credit reward or fixed price before you agree.

Saving your flexibility never authorizes a booking change. You choose which offers to accept. For a request, approve the outcome, quoted price and expiry in advance. Flex can act only within those terms.

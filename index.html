Cathay Flex

A marketplace that turns passenger flexibility into value.

Team Globe Cathay Hackathon 2026

Passengers value different things about a journey. Those differences create room for exchanges that can make travel work better for everyone.

I conceived Cathay Flex as a marketplace that recognises this value. It brings passenger preferences and airline constraints together to identify voluntary exchanges, helping scarce travel resources reach the people who value them most.

Value beyond a single journey

Flexibility can span different resources and different points in time. A traveller could exchange one travel benefit for another, or receive Flex credits for an agreed change and use that value on a future journey.

Matching around what matters

We are developing matching methods and AI-assisted interfaces that let travellers describe what matters to them. These needs become preferences and constraints, helping the system propose options that passengers can choose while respecting what the airline can deliver.
